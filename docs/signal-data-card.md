**Series and unit.** DGS10 is the 10-year constant-maturity Treasury yield, daily, percent per annum, from the Federal Reserve Board's H.15 release. [FRED series](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10) · [ALFRED series](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/series?seid=DGS10)

**Availability.** The app selects the last nonmissing observation recorded in each archived vintage. The September 30 vintage ends September 29 at 5.26%. The October 29 vintage ends October 28 at 5.05%. The observed lag is one business day in both examples. The Lab 17 comparison recorded zero revisions on overlapping observations, with 20 newer observations in the later data. [Lab 17 data card](https://github.com/yu1260/Project-2-FIN439/blob/e1c3ed89daf6c7a5e051b1bf942f9e2f88ddbf95/asof_data_card.md)

**Snapshot and use.** The two rows are transcribed from the October 29, 2026 Lab 17 download evidence. The September vintage is available for the October 22 portfolio evidence date. The October 29 vintage illustrates a later information boundary and is available only under its own selected date. Treat the yield as rate-risk monitoring context for GOVT and IGIB. The solver uses the adjusted-price sample independently of the yield; no signal-conditioned return or timing claim is made.
